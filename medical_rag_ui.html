What are the common symptoms of COPD?
👤
🩺
Common symptoms of COPD include persistent cough, increased mucus production, shortness of breath, wheezing, chest tightness, and reduced exercise tolerance. Symptoms often worsen gradually over time. 1 2

If symptoms are severe, rapidly worsening, or associated with low oxygen levels, urgent medical evaluation may be needed.
